Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 25, 2026

Simple Text Message (“the App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, used, and handled when you use the App.

1. SMS and Messaging Data

Simple Text Message is designed to function as an SMS messaging application. Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, the App may access SMS/MMS messages on your device to provide messaging functionality, including viewing conversations, sending and receiving messages, searching messages, scheduling messages, archiving conversations, starring messages, blocking numbers, and related messaging features.

Your SMS/MMS message content is processed on your device for the App's messaging functionality.

We do not sell your private SMS/MMS content.

2. Contacts

The App may request access to your device contacts so that it can display contact names, phone numbers, and related information when you send or receive messages.

Contact information is used to provide messaging functionality and is not sold by us.

3. Phone Numbers and SMS Permissions

The App may request permissions necessary for SMS functionality, including permissions to read, receive, and send SMS messages.

These permissions are used only to provide features that require them.

4. Default SMS App

Some functionality may require Simple Text Message to be selected as your device's default SMS application.

Android may ask you to approve this change. You can change your default SMS application through your device settings.

5. Scheduled Messages

If you schedule an SMS, the App may store the necessary message information locally on your device until the scheduled message is processed.

6. Advertising

The App may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may collect or process certain information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from network information, ad interactions, and diagnostic information, subject to their own privacy policies and your consent choices where required.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. Firebase Services

The App may use Google Firebase services, including Firebase Remote Config, to remotely configure certain application features and settings.

8. Premium / No Ads Subscription

Simple Text Message may offer a paid subscription or purchase that removes advertisements.

Purchases and subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing. We do not directly collect or store your complete payment card or banking details.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell your private SMS messages or contacts.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no electronic system or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

12. App Permissions

  • Reading SMS messages
  • Sending SMS messages
  • Receiving SMS messages
  • Accessing contacts
  • Notifications
  • Scheduling functionality

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version may be made available through the App or the page where this Privacy Policy is published.
